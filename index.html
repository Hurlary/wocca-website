Measured. Verified. Certified.

WOCCA is the Water-Offset Conservation & Certification Agency, an independent organization establishing a rigorous framework for measuring, verifying, and certifying water conservation. It gives legislators, regulators, communities, and businesses a tool to address new water demand in water-scarce areas without increasing overall water consumption, while still allowing economic growth. The goal is to reach net-zero water use and, from there, move toward becoming water positive.

We connect organizations seeking to address their water use with conservation projects capable of producing measurable water savings.

Through clear standards, independent verification, and transparent certification, WOCCA helps ensure that recognized water offsets represent real, documented conservation outcomes.

WHO WE ARE

An independent agency for water conservation integrity.

WOCCA exists because water stress is a local problem with global consequences. As regions face growing demand and shrinking supply, we give regulators, developers, and communities a shared, credible standard for what a genuine water conservation outcome looks like.

  • Independent, science-based governance
  • Standards built with hydrologists and policy experts
  • Public, auditable certification records
About WOCCA

OUR MISSION

Net-zero water, then water positive.

Our mission is to build the standards infrastructure that lets growth and conservation coexist: every unit of new water demand matched by a verified unit of water saved, restored, or recycled elsewhere in the same basin.

Read our mission

HOW IT WORKS

A three-stage path from claim to certificate.

Every water offset recognized by WOCCA passes through the same disciplined sequence, so a certificate always means the same thing.

01

Measure

Project developers quantify water savings using WOCCA-approved methodologies specific to the conservation activity — from irrigation efficiency to leak reduction and greywater reuse.

02

Verify

Independent, accredited verifiers review project data, site evidence, and metering records against the standard before any claim is accepted.

03

Certify

Verified savings are issued as WOCCA Water Offset Certificates, recorded in a public registry to prevent double-counting and support long-term accountability.
