FAQ

Before you reach out.

How quickly will I hear back?

Our standards team reviews inquiries and routes them to the right specialist based on what you're looking to do.

Do you work outside a single region?

Methodologies are designed to be adaptable across basins; reach out and we can talk through your specific location.

Is there a cost to certify a project?

Costs are primarily verification fees paid to the accredited verifier you engage; get in touch for guidance on what to expect.
